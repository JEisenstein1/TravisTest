Chart Builder — ColoriTech card

Card geometry and colour patches are already measured. This tool captures the per-analyte level colours that turn readings into results.
How to use
1. Dip a strip and photograph card + strip at exactly the same 90-second mark the app uses — timing bias here poisons every future reading.
2. Load the photo. It auto-detects, rectifies, colour-corrects, and reads all 10 pads.
3. For each analyte whose true level you know on that strip, pick the level and capture.
4. Repeat across several strips per level; samples are averaged.
5. Export and paste into REFERENCE_CHART in card-calibration.js.
Free baseline: a fresh dry or water-dipped strip gives every analyte its negative/lowest level at once — use the button below.
